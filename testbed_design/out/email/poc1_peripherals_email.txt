Subject: PoC 1 (splitter bench) - peripheral equipment list and open questions

Hi all,

Below is the peripheral equipment needed for PoC 1, the conducted bench in which the hub's W and E modules are shared through RF splitters. The list leaves out the devices themselves: the Siklu Spider boxes / N367 nodes, the eight Ceragon IP-50CX radios, and the servers, traffic hosts, agent PCs and bench PC with its monitor.

The quantities come from the bench's digital twin. Cable lengths are the routed paths plus about 10 % slack. The list is tentative, and the questions at the end change some lines. Please mark what we already have, and I will turn the rest into an order.

Open questions (the answers change the list)
1. Spider box DC terminal block: what voltage and polarity does it take, and does it power the nodes or only the fans and the Raspberry Pi? If it powers the nodes, the DC/PoE adaptors, their PoE cords and 6 breaker feeds go away. A photo of each box's rear would settle this, and also show which RJ-45 is ETH1.
2. GNSS: do the N367 nodes need an external antenna, or is their internal GPS enough? If it is enough, section 7 is dropped.
3. Programmable attenuators (9 needed): which model do we buy? I assumed 4 channels, 0-63 dB, to 18 GHz, a small box with a dial. I also need its power input (brick or C14), its control interface (USB or LAN) and its size.
4. IP-50CX band: if the radios are 23 GHz, the J7204B and the WR-62 parts do not work, and the 18-26.5 GHz set is needed. Does a DC connector ship with each radio, and what is the waveguide / antenna interface?
5. The 10 "RF cables" on hand: what type and length are they? They could cover part of the 88 SMA cables.
6. The Raspberry Pi inside each Spider box: does it need a LAN connection? That would add 4 ports, and the management switch already uses 29 of its 30, so we would need a larger one.
7. Room layout: where does the bench table stand relative to the rack, and which wall sockets are there? I assumed 4 x IEC 60309 16 A sockets (3 PDUs + the table strip) and a lab earth bar near the rack.
8. Switched PDUs: does the lab already have PDUs that can switch each outlet remotely? The t0 event cuts power at 5 outlets, so this is required.
