Subject: PoC 1 (splitter bench) - peripheral equipment list and open questions

Hi all,

Below is the peripheral equipment needed for PoC 1, the conducted bench where the hub's W and E sectors are shared through RF splitters. The list leaves out the devices themselves: the Siklu Spider boxes / N367 nodes, the eight Ceragon IP-50CX radios, and the servers, traffic hosts, agent PCs and the bench PC with its monitor.

Key point: the scenario only needs links to go down, and that happens because whole devices are switched off at t0 (switched PDU outlets cut the hub node, the two CORE radios and the two servers). No link has to be faded, so the bench needs NO programmable attenuators. Fixed SMA pads set the path loss on every link, and the three MARL bridges are held down in Terragraph and enabled when MARL engages them. This removes the 9 attenuator boxes and the Keysight J7204B, frees 10U of rack space, 10 power outlets and 10 switch ports, and cuts the SMA cables from 88 to 48.

The quantities come from the bench's digital twin, regenerated with the pads in place. Cable lengths are the routed paths plus about 10 % slack. Please mark what we already have, and I will turn the rest into an order.

Open questions (the answers change the list)
1. MARL bridges: is it acceptable to emulate MARL engaging a bridge by bringing that link up in Terragraph (the link sits behind fixed pads, held down until then)? The E2E controller is one of the devices switched off at t0, so after t0 the bridge cannot be enabled through it. My plan: before t0 the three bridges are ignition-disabled on the controller; after t0 the MARL agent of the bridge's A-end node associates the link directly at node level (Terragraph minion command, tg2 minion assoc). Does the N367 firmware accept that with no controller running? If not, the fallback needs no new parts: leave the E2E server's PDU outlet (PDU-C.O2) on at t0, so the controller stays up and MARL enables the bridge's ignition through it; the scenario then loses only its controller-down part. If we want the change to happen at RF level instead, we need 3 programmable attenuators (one 4-channel box per bridge).
2. IP-50 hops: the bench has no Keysight J7204B. Each hop is a fixed 70 dB chain and goes down only when CORE's radios lose power at t0 (W1-W2 and E1-E2 never change). Please confirm DPIR does not need to see an IP-50 hop degrade (slow fades) before it fails; if it does, a 4-channel programmable attenuator goes back on the table for those hops.
3. Spider box DC header: the rear photo shows a green 4-pole pluggable header with a screw flange on each box, no plug fitted. What does it feed (the nodes, or only the fans and the Raspberry Pi), at what voltage, and which pin is which? What is its pitch (5.08 or 7.62 mm)? If it powers the nodes, the 6 DC/PoE adaptors, their PoE cords and the hub's PoE injector go away, and the hub box gets its own switched 48 V supply instead. Also: which of the two RJ-45 ports in each node window is ETH1?
4. GNSS: do the N367 nodes need an external antenna, or is their internal GPS enough? If it is enough, section 7 is dropped. The hub box shows no SMA next to its node window in the photo: does the hub node take GNSS at all? If not, 6 jumpers instead of 7.
5. IP-50CX band: if the radios are 23 GHz, the WR-62 transitions and the 18 GHz pads do not fit, and the 18-26.5 GHz parts are needed. Does a DC connector ship with each radio, and what is the waveguide / antenna interface?
6. The 10 "RF cables" on hand: what type and length are they? They could cover part of the 48 SMA cables.
7. The Raspberry Pi inside each Spider box: does it need a LAN connection? The management switch has room for the 4 extra ports.
8. Room layout: where does the bench table stand relative to the rack, and which wall sockets are there? I assumed 4 x IEC 60309 16 A sockets (3 PDUs + the table strip) and a lab earth bar near the rack.
9. Switched PDUs: does the lab already have PDUs that can switch each outlet remotely? The t0 event cuts power at 5 outlets, so this is required.
